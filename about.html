WHO WE ARE

From printing roots to an integrated manufacturing network.

Fashionmill Co. was founded in 1985. The business evolved through successive stages of printing, garment manufacturing, textiles, ready-made apparel, embroidery and fabric supply. That journey shaped how we approach production today: understand the product, coordinate the right processes and deliver against the buyer’s requirements.

Our head office is in Dokki, Giza, while our garment and textile manufacturing facilities operate in Shubra El Kheima, Qalyubia. Our combined workforce across those two facilities is 185 people.
